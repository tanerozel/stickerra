Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Türkçe

Stickerra ("the app") is a sticker maker for Android published by inCODE. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Photos, videos and stickers

  • Images and videos you pick are processed on your device only. Background removal uses Google ML Kit's on-device model; your images are not uploaded to us or to Google.
  • Stickers and packs are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) reads the sticker files from the app through Android's content provider mechanism.
  • If you create a backup, the .zip file is written to the location you choose.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app has no login and does not collect your name, email, contacts or phone number. It contains no advertising, no advertising SDK and no analytics, and it does not access your advertising ID.

Permissions

The app uses the system photo picker, so it does not request storage, camera, contacts or keyboard permissions. It uses the internet only to download the on-device AI model and the optional ready-made sticker packs.

Purchase

Stickerra is a paid app: you pay once on Google Play when you install it. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Ready-made sticker packs

When you open the app or tap "Download", it fetches the pack list and pack files from this website (GitHub Pages). This is a plain file download: no account or personal data is sent, but like any web request GitHub receives your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

All data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Backups you exported must be deleted by you.
